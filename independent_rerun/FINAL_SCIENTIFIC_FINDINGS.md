# Post-review execution findings

Scientific Reports submission `417743dc-6d78-4ff8-9321-a80ca41e855e`. These are post-review analyses and are not prespecified primary experiments. The archived manuscript and Paper 2 were not edited.

## Completed fair neural comparison

The new Kaggle experiment ran all 12 locked independent DGP seeds under `NO_INCREMENTAL_MOLECULAR_SIGNAL` and `JOINT_MOLECULAR_SYNERGY`, with one fixed patient TEST split and one network seed. Each DGP seed is the independent unit within a mechanism; the two regimes are evaluated separately. The same all-modality TRAIN nuisance pseudo-outcomes were used for CM-OSR, Uniform Routing and both bagged early-fusion comparators. Calibration, when shown, was chosen by observed DEV R-loss without true DEV CATE or TEST. The primary table below uses raw predictions.

| Mechanism | CM-OSR | Uniform same experts | Bagged early fusion, 20 fits | Full X | Clinical X (descriptive) | Zero effect |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No incremental molecular signal | 0.720 | 1.644 | 2.670 | 0.149 | 0.097 | 0.147 |
| Joint molecular synergy | 0.711 | 1.681 | 2.676 | 0.147 | 0.108 | 0.156 |

Numbers are mean Root-PEHE across 12 independent DGP seeds per mechanism. Full X, Clinical X/T, and zero-effect values come from the pre-existing 12-seed classical runs, with exact matching TEST indices and source data hash. The new runs' true-CATE mean and standard deviation matched the archived values; zero-effect Root-PEHE differed by at most 1.09e-9, consistent with floating-point changes. Clinical-only X/T do not by themselves adjust for all simulated confounding variables and should not be interpreted as a valid causal-adjustment comparator; Full X/T are the relevant all-modality classical comparators.

Lower Root-PEHE for the zero-effect predictor does not mean a better treatment policy: its mean policy regret was 0.103 and 0.105, compared with 0.049 and 0.042 for CM-OSR and 0.024 and 0.025 for Full X. Clipped Root-PEHE, out-of-range fractions and full uncertainty summaries are in `FAIR_NEURAL_SUMMARY.csv`.

The paired CM-OSR minus 20-fit bagged difference was -1.950 (95% t interval -2.192 to -1.709) without incremental molecular signal and -1.965 (-2.263 to -1.668) under joint synergy. CM-OSR minus Uniform Routing using the same experts was -0.924 (-1.038 to -0.809) and -0.970 (-1.151 to -0.789), respectively. However, CM-OSR minus Full X was +0.571 (0.425 to 0.716) and +0.564 (0.413 to 0.715). Negative differences favor CM-OSR. The data support a within-expert learned-routing contrast against uniform averaging, but do not establish a general advantage of CM-OSR over simpler valid all-modality causal learners or identify Routing as the sole cause of any neural contrast.

The 20-fit bag used four repeated five-fold TRAIN fits with a 150-epoch cap and the same TRAIN-MSE stopping rule. CM-OSR used 24 expert fits and a router. Across the 24 DGP-regime runs, the mean ratio of bagged to CM expert optimizer steps was 1.017, and the ratio of deployed parameters was 1.082. Mean recorded training time was 40.82 seconds for the bag versus 50.93 seconds for CM-OSR (mean paired ratio 0.807); wall time was not forced to equality. The training objectives and fit counts remain different. The preceding 25-fit resource pilot is retained separately: it used about twice the CM optimizer steps and 35% more parameters on the examined seed, so it is not presented as compute matched. All raw and DEV-calibrated outcomes, including unfavorable outcomes, are in `FAIR_NEURAL_COMPARISON.csv`.

The 20-fit training budget was chosen after inspecting the one-seed 25-fit pilot, whose TEST outcomes were already available. The change was motivated by measured optimizer-step and parameter imbalances, but the resulting 12-seed comparison remains an exploratory post-review analysis. Its intervals quantify seed variation conditional on this chosen protocol and do not correct for protocol adaptation.

The observed DEV R-loss selected scale zero for 92 of 96 neural model/regime/seed evaluations; the four nonzero selections were CM-OSR at scale 0.25. These calibrated predictions often approach the zero-effect Root-PEHE but can have worse policy regret. The calibration rule and its null selections are reported as a negative result, not used to choose a favorable TEST result.

## Independent Stage 4 retraining

The first archived Stage 4 pair, DGP 101 / network seed 17, was actually retrained on Kaggle with the original benchmark code and the private processed data (SHA-256 `e94166a00268aab5cb99cb74866ed81771a96ba88c9dc4368107bd0795081e1a`). Against the 45 archived per-run numeric metric entries, the GPU rerun had 5 failures under the existing RTOL 1e-3 / ATOL 1e-6, while the CPU rerun had 22. The GPU differences were confined to X-learner and R-learner metrics; individual differences are in `INDEPENDENT_RERUN_COMPARISON.json`. This is a **FAIL for strict numeric reproduction**, even though training completed. No new full-grid PRIMARY_GATE decision is inferred from one run.

A fresh CPU Kaggle session restored the private metrics-only checkpoint, skipped DGP101/NET17 and trained NET29 and NET43. The `RESTORED_CHECKPOINT` and `SKIP` entries in its log verify resumption across sessions. The full frozen Stage 4 grid then completed in five separate Kaggle kernels: 15 seed pairs forming five independent DGP clusters, with three network seeds nested within each DGP. The newly aggregated `PRIMARY_GATE` was **PASS**, matching the archived decision. Its mean CM-OSR minus early-fusion Root-PEHE was **-1.980784** (95% cluster bootstrap interval **-2.201851 to -1.722442**), versus archived **-1.967963** (**-2.199146 to -1.691623**). The observed mean difference was -0.012821; the gate is stable, while exact metric reproduction is not.

All 15 CPU per-run comparisons failed the documented RTOL 1e-3 / ATOL 1e-6. In the corrected aggregate table comparison, 333 of 1125 numerical entries in `ALL_CORE_METRICS.csv` and 122 of 270 in `PRIMARY_PAIRED_REPLICATES.csv` differed beyond tolerance. The first Kaggle comparator also misaligned some aggregate rows by sorting on text fields alone. A post-training local comparator repair aligned by DGP seed, network seed and method; both original and corrected comparator reports are retained. No model was retrained for that repair. `PRIMARY_GATE.json` has no categorical decision mismatch, but its mean and interval values exceed the strict numerical tolerance. The CPU Kaggle runtime used Python 3.13.18, NumPy 2.1.3, scikit-learn 1.6.1 and Torch 2.11.0+cpu; this differs from the archived environment and may contribute to numerical variation, but is not established as its sole cause.

## Additional frozen-stage decisions

The **full Stage 6A DEV-only grid** was independently rerun: 10 frozen DGP/network pairs. Its new decision is `CANDIDATE_FROZEN_FOR_STAGE6B`, with `AOSR_X_gamma2` again selected; both match the archive. The selection record confirms `candidate_selection_split=DEV_ONLY` and `test_accessed_for_selection=false`. Its null-guard threshold shifted from 0.2185570285 to 0.2184967861 and passes the documented RTOL 1e-3 / ATOL 1e-6. All 10 per-run comparisons failed strict numerical matching; the seed/model-keyed aggregate has 2170 failing numerical cells among 4050 comparisons, with largest absolute difference 0.177948. The frozen candidate decision is stable, but the underlying metrics do not reproduce exactly.

The **full Stage 6B confirmatory grid** was rerun: 15 frozen DGP/network pairs, with three network seeds nested within each of five DGP seeds. Its new decision remains `CONFIRMATORY_MIXED_OR_FAIL`, matching the archive. All 15 fresh run manifests show `AOSR_X_gamma2`, `candidate_retuned=false`, `test_used_for_selection=false`, and `evaluation_split=TEST_ONCE`; gate 5 (support selectivity) failed while the other listed gates passed. Stage 6B used the **archived pre-TEST Stage 6A selection** as its frozen dependency, recorded by SHA-256 in `RUN_MANIFESTS/STAGE6B/frozen_archived_stage6a_selection_dependency.json`. The independent Stage 6A rerun later selected the same candidate; Stage 6B was not retuned or rerun in response. All 15 per-run comparisons failed strict numeric matching. Its keyed aggregate table has 2355 failing numerical cells among 5400 comparisons, with largest absolute difference 0.51749. The categorical decision matched, but three numerical fields in the decision JSON did not. The negative confirmatory conclusion remains, without exact numerical reproduction.

The **full Stage 6C mechanism grid** was rerun: 10 frozen DGP/network pairs. Its new decision remains `WEAK_METHOD_INCREMENT_DO_NOT_POSITION_AS_PRIMARY_NOVEL_TECHNIQUE`, matching the archive. H2 and H4 passed; H1, H3 (learned routing), H5 and H6 failed. All 10 per-run comparisons failed strict numerical matching. The keyed aggregate method/regime table has 1105 failing numerical cells among 2310 comparisons, with largest absolute difference 0.07219. The categorical decision and all four numerical fields in the decision JSON passed the documented tolerance. No general routing benefit is established.

The **full Stage 7B development grid** was independently rerun: 12 frozen DGP/learner pairs on TRAIN/DEV only. Its new decision remains `STAGE7B_DEVELOPMENT_NOT_STRONG_ENOUGH_STOP_BEFORE_CONFIRMATORY`, matching the archive. Gates D1, D2 and D3 passed; D4, D5 and D6 failed. No molecular candidate was certified, and incremental acceptance was zero. The categorical decision and numerical summary passed the documented tolerance, but all 12 per-run metric tables failed strict numerical matching and the aggregate method/regime table had 64 mismatched cells. A first one-pair pilot was repeated once because the original aggregator needed individual DEV predictions missing from its private checkpoint; the repeat retained that file only in private Kaggle storage. The distributable ZIP excludes it. The original Kaggle comparator report and corrected seed-key comparison are both retained.

All stage decisions and numerical checks are separate in `VALIDATION_REPORT.json`, `STAGE_DECISION_SUMMARY.csv`, and `NUMERIC_DIFFERENCE_SUMMARY.csv`. One local comparator revision briefly treated blank-capable numerical metrics as row keys; this was repaired and all saved checkpoints were revalidated without training. `COMPARATOR_VALIDATION.json` tests keyed alignment and metric-change detection. No RTOL/ATOL limits were widened, TEST was not used to reselect AOSR, and unfavorable outcomes remain in the package.
