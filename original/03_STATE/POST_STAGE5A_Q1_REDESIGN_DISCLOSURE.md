# POST-STAGE5A METHOD DEVELOPMENT DISCLOSURE

Stage 5A is frozen and preserved as the evaluation of CM-OSR v1.

The Stage 5A evidence showed:
- strong superiority to capacity-matched early-fusion and generic multimodal DR controls;
- useful support-aware selective behavior;
- approximately nominal semi-synthetic coverage;
- materially weaker null-effect behavior than X/T learners;
- X/T learners remained stronger point estimators in several regimes.

Therefore any method change after Stage 5A is explicitly **post-result method development**.
It must not be described as prespecified before Stage 5A, and it cannot replace the
original primary result.

Stage 6A is a NEW development phase that uses only fresh semi-synthetic DGP seeds and
the original DEV split. The original TEST split is forbidden for candidate selection.

One candidate may be frozen after Stage 6A and evaluated once in Stage 6B on separately
locked fresh DGP seeds and TEST. CHER-LOB and TRIO remain the real-trial validation path.
